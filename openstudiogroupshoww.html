Open Studio Group Show
During the open studio day and Sommerfest of the AdW 11 studiohouse
Saturday 29.8.2026 15 - 22
Auf dem Wolf 11, Basel
Four artists that have a studio at the studiohouse Adw 11 each invited one artist from outside of Basel. The show takes place in a studio room that two people only recently have started renting and in which they have barely started to move into. We decided not to remove a table and a few objects that were already in the room.
with works by
Nicolas Spycher and Matthias Holznagel
Nina Brügger and Laura Veenemans
Sebastiaan Maes and Jan Prahm Miró
Olivia Vidovic and Rosa Jaranovic
curated by
Olivia Vidovic
